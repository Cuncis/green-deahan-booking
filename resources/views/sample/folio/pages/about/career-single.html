Machine Learning Specialist

Apply now

We are looking for a talented Machine Learning Specialist to join our growing data science team.

You'll design, build, and deploy machine learning models that power our products, working closely with engineering and product teams to translate business problems into data-driven solutions.

Principal responsibilities
  • Model development: Design and train machine learning models for prediction and classification tasks.
  • Data pipelines: Build robust pipelines to collect, clean, and process large datasets.
  • Collaboration: Work with cross-functional teams to integrate models into production.
Qualifications
  • Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, or related field.
  • Experience: 3+ years of experience building and deploying machine learning models.
  • Skills: Proficiency in Python, SQL, and common ML frameworks.
What we offer
  • Compensation: Competitive salary and equity package.
  • Flexibility: Remote-friendly work with flexible hours.
  • Growth: Learning budget and mentorship opportunities.
How to apply

If you are excited about the opportunity to make a significant impact through machine learning, we would love to hear from you!
